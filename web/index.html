    how not to fall off the world

    1. the world is flat and balanced on a pin. every settler you drop costs the same flat stake in $wif, and pulls the world toward its spot.
    2. further out pays more. rings pay ×1 near the pole up to ×5 on the ice wall, and outer settlers pull the world much harder.
    3. when the round ends, the world tips. the low side's edge slides off first: the steeper the tip, the further in the slide reaches. the red zone shows who would fall right now. the survivors split what fell, weighted by their ring.
    4. the candle. the last part of every round hides the real end. drop too late and your settler lands after the candle went out: it falls too.
    5. flat day. if the world ends almost perfectly level (and 3+ players are on it), nobody falls and the survivors share half the flat jackpot.
    6. everyone pays the same. max 3 settlers per wallet per round. 5% of everything that falls is burned forever, 5% feeds the jackpot.

    fully onchain on robinhood chain: no server decides anything. the contract has no owner. $wif is a memecoin with no promise of value; play with what you can afford to lose.
